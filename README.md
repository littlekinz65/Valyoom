An On-Screen assistive touch control to Adjust Volume, Brightness and much more for Android Devices.
Very helpful for the mobile users with defective Volume and Lock Buttons, Triggers Instantly, Lightweight, does not consume Much of your RAM and Do Not drain your Battery.

# A Floating Assistive Button without missing the immersive Viewing Experience.
* Volume Control
* Brightness Control (inbuilt from Valyoom)
* Screenshot
* Mute
* ScreenLock
* Hide Floating Button
